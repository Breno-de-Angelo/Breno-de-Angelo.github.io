Side projects & coursework

A selection from GitHub: personal projects, and course projects that went further than they had to.

Audio & embedded

Rust · DSP · egui · Android

Virtual guitar amp

A real-time guitar effects processor: the signal runs through a configurable chain of 17 DSP pedals (distortion, compressor, wah, chorus, phaser, pitch shifter, looper…) and an impulse-response cabinet simulator, with 20 factory presets, a YIN-based tuner, a metronome and a live oscilloscope/FFT view. Runs on desktop and Android.

 C · EFM32 bare metal

Embedded drum machine

Drum machine on an EFM32 Giant Gecko with hand-written drivers: WAV samples converted to C arrays, three-voice mixing, BPM control, and audio out via DAC, PWM or I²S.

Robotics

Python · ROS 2 · Nav2 · YOLO

Autonomous explorer

A simulated TurtleBot3 with 3D lidar explores an unknown world, choosing its next goals from the Nav2 costmap, and locates objects by combining a fine-tuned YOLO detector with lidar, estimating each object's width.

 Python · ROS 2 · Nav2

Person-following robot

A simulated robot explores until it detects a person, then stops exploring and navigates to them with Nav2.

 Python · ROS · Control

Quadrotor flight control

Inner/outer-loop (kinematic + dynamic) controller for a Parrot Bebop 2, tuned in simulation and then flown on the real drone for positioning, trajectory tracking and path following.

Computer vision & ML

Python · YOLOv8 · MQTT

Occupancy-based power switching

One hallway camera tracks people with YOLOv8 and counts them in and out of each door marked on the frame; over MQTT, each room's power is switched on when someone is inside and off when it empties.

 Python · PyTorch

Skin-lesion classifier generalization

Trains EfficientNet on PAD-UFES-20 and on ISIC 2019+2020, then tests each on the other dataset to measure how well it transfers (balanced accuracy, ROC curves, W&B tracking).

Algorithms

Python · Algorithms

Graph algorithms & scalability

Edge-disjoint paths via Menger's theorem and Dinic's max-flow in O(E√E), with generated graphs and empirical time and memory scaling fits.

 C

Huffman compressor

File encoder and decoder in plain C, verified by a lossless round trip on the full text of the Bible.

Also on GitHub: a ROS 2 diff-drive robot with EKF odometry and SLAM Toolbox, an Ackermann vehicle in Gazebo, numerical ODE solvers in Octave, 8086 assembly, C data structures, and my Sway dotfiles.
