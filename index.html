01 Research

Deep learning for genomics · 2026 – present

Reading phenotypes out of DNA with AlphaGenome

My M.Sc. asks how neural genome models can point to markers associated with human traits, with longevity as the long-term target. We feed DNA through Google DeepMind's AlphaGenome and learn on top of its predicted functional signals.

  • Ancestry from 11 pigmentation genes (ICANN 2026, oral, presented by me). Using predicted RNA-seq over 11 pigmentation genes for 2,237 individuals from the 1000 Genomes Project, a CNN classifies five superpopulations at 84% accuracy, versus 81% for XGBoost and 69% for Random Forest. I built both tree baselines and ran the hyperparameter search for every model. A single known pigmentation gene (OCA2) reaches 64%; a random gene (FBXO5) gets 37%.
  • Gene prioritization (ongoing). Using AlphaGenome to prioritize the genes associated with a phenotype of interest.
  • Virtual Embryo Challenge. Part of the LCAD team, with my advisor and lab colleagues. I helped frame the problem and design the models.

Humanoid robot learning · B.Sc. thesis 2025 · ongoing

Prometheus, teaching a Unitree G1 humanoid by VR teleoperation

My undergraduate thesis evaluated whether adding depth helps an Action Chunking Transformer (ACT) policy manipulate objects with a humanoid.

  • Extended an open-source Meta Quest 3 teleoperation stack with a new Pinch Retargeting hand-control strategy and stereoscopic feedback.
  • Collected pick-and-place demonstrations and curated the 30 best (rated on a 5-point scale) into a LeRobot dataset.
  • Trained RGB-only and depth-augmented (PointNet encoder) ACT policies.
  • Now supervising undergraduates who continue the project: benchmarking vision-language-action (VLA) models, collecting new data, and writing a hierarchical program for the robot to make a cup of coffee on its own.

Mobile manipulation · LLMs · 2022 – 2025

Hercules, an autonomous forklift you talk to

A team effort at LCAD: an Ackermann robot with a manipulator arm that moves cellulose bales from a pile onto a truck in a scaled warehouse, taking orders spoken in natural language and interpreted by an LLM.

  • Programmed the arm motion and designed the low-level control system, then integrated it into the autonomy stack.
  • Designed the bale-recognition system: YOLOv8 fine-tuned on 859 images plus 3D localization from a depth camera, in ROS 2.
  • Contributed to the chat-based command interface. In our benchmark GPT-4 approved 100% of feasible tasks and rejected 83.3% of unfeasible ones; the best open model (Mixtral) managed 53.3% and 23.3%.
  • First-author paper in JISEM (2025).

02 Industry

AUMO S.A. · Employee · 2023 – present

Backend, DevOps and project management for enterprise LLM products

AUMO is a startup born in the UFES incubator. It sells SaaS and on-premise chatbots grounded in company knowledge through retrieval-augmented generation, with connectors to Google Drive, OneDrive, Jira and others, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, and custom small language models trained per contract. There I'm responsible for the Python backend, the infrastructure (DevOps) and project management.

  • Semantic routing (SBSI 2026, oral, first author). I led the research on a layer that sorts incoming chat messages into relevant, unrelated, chitchat or spam before anything reaches the LLM. On a balanced set of 6,760 Portuguese messages, an embedding-similarity router hit 98.5% accuracy (BERT: 99.0%) against 53.8% for zero-shot GPT, cutting cost by 96% (US$18.10 to US$0.32 per million requests) and memory from 6.5 GB to 1.0 GB.

04 Side projects & coursework

Python · ROS 2 · Nav2 · YOLO

Autonomous explorer

A simulated TurtleBot3 with 3D lidar explores an unknown world, choosing its next goals from the Nav2 costmap, and locates objects by combining a fine-tuned YOLO detector with lidar, estimating each object's width.

 Python · ROS · Control

Quadrotor flight control

Inner/outer-loop (kinematic + dynamic) controller for a Parrot Bebop 2, tuned in simulation and then flown on the real drone for positioning, trajectory tracking and path following.

 Python · YOLOv8 · MQTT

Occupancy-based power switching

One hallway camera tracks people with YOLOv8 and counts them in and out of each door marked on the frame; over MQTT, each room's power is switched on when someone is inside and off when it empties.

 Python · ROS 2 · Nav2

Person-following robot

A simulated robot explores until it detects a person, then stops exploring and navigates to them with Nav2.

 Python · PyTorch
